Coverage extension settings

Dashboard address

Where your Coverage dashboard is. Use http://localhost:3000 while developing.

Application sites

"Fill selected" on your dashboard opens each job in a background tab and fills it, which needs access to that application site (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever…). Coverage only reads and fills application forms there, and never submits.

Applications filled at once in the background. Each one is a full browser tab (a Workday form takes several hundred MB), so fewer is lighter on your computer; finished Workday tabs are let go when Chrome needs the memory.

Saved logins optional

Lets Coverage sign in to application accounts you already have (each bank's Workday is its own account), so a batch fill doesn't stop at every login. Off by default.

  • Encrypted with your PIN; unlocked once per browser session, locked again when the browser closes.
  • Used only on the exact site you saved it for, only in a sign-in form, by fixed rules.
  • No AI ever sees it, and it never leaves this browser (not to your dashboard, not to any server).
  • Never creates accounts, and never reads Chrome's own saved passwords (Chrome doesn't allow that).

    Gmail open tracking test build

    Adds Coverage's open-tracking image to emails you send from Gmail itself (new emails, replies, forwards, Schedule send), so Email tracking on your dashboard lists them too. In your Sent list, a ✓ means tracked and ✓✓ means opened. Your own views of those emails are recognized and never count as opens. Needs your Coverage tab to have tracking on; the extension only reads the compose window you send from and the Sent list.

    Auto mode test build

    Coverage stops asking. On each application it presses the job page's Apply (or the AI agent finds the way in), creates an account when the site wants one (a strong password, saved in your saved logins first), signs in with it next time, ticks the data-privacy, terms and consent boxes, fills sites that aren't on your tracked jobs without checking with you, and goes page after page. It still stops at the final Submit: you read the review page and submit it yourself.

    Ticking consent and certification boxes ("I agree to the privacy notice", "the information is true and complete") agrees to that site's terms and privacy policy on your behalf. Your saved logins stay unlocked on this computer (anyone using this Chrome profile could use them). Your call.

    LinkedIn job search test build

    Lets "Search LinkedIn Jobs" on your dashboard open a LinkedIn Jobs search in your browser, read each posting at a person's pace (up to 60 a day) and add them to your finds. It only reads job postings, never people, and stops at any LinkedIn sign-in or security check.

    LinkedIn's User Agreement forbids automated access, even from your own browser. LinkedIn can restrict or ban the account it runs on. Your account, your call.

    Handshake job search test build

    Lets "Search Handshake" on your dashboard open a Handshake job search in your browser, read each posting at a person's pace (up to 30 a run, 90 a day) and hand them to your dashboard, where the AI reviews them. During Fill selected it presses "Apply externally" to get to the bank's own form. It never uses Handshake's own Apply, never messages anyone, and stops at any sign-in.

    Handshake's terms forbid automated access, even from your own browser. Handshake can restrict the account it runs on. Your account, your call.

    AI agent for applications test build

    After the rules fill what they can (and attach your resume), the model you picked in the agent test bench (popup → Agent test bench) finishes the application: it reads the page (and a screenshot when you watch), adds your experience, answers the rest from your Coverage info and resume, and presses Next. It stops before the final Submit, never creates accounts, and never leaves the application. Your phone, email and street address are typed by the extension, not sent to the model. Uses your own API key; each step is one model call.

    Vision helper (optional): a model that can read screenshots, used only for the steps where the agent needs to look at the page (DeepSeek can't read images). Its key comes from the agent test bench.

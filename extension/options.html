Coverage extension settings

Dashboard address

Where your Coverage dashboard is. Use http://localhost:3000 while developing.

Application sites

"Fill selected" on your dashboard opens each job in a background tab and fills it, which needs access to that application site (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever…). Coverage only reads and fills application forms there, and never submits.

Saved logins optional

Lets Coverage sign in to application accounts you already have (each bank's Workday is its own account), so a batch fill doesn't stop at every login. Off by default.

  • Encrypted with your PIN; unlocked once per browser session, locked again when the browser closes.
  • Used only on the exact site you saved it for, only in a sign-in form, by fixed rules.
  • No AI ever sees it, and it never leaves this browser (not to your dashboard, not to any server).
  • Never creates accounts, and never reads Chrome's own saved passwords (Chrome doesn't allow that).

    LinkedIn job search test build

    Lets "Search LinkedIn Jobs" on your dashboard open a LinkedIn Jobs search in your browser, read each posting at a person's pace (up to 60 a day) and add them to your finds. It only reads job postings, never people, and stops at any LinkedIn sign-in or security check.

    LinkedIn's User Agreement forbids automated access, even from your own browser. LinkedIn can restrict or ban the account it runs on. Your account, your call.
